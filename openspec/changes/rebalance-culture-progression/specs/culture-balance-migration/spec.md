## Purpose

Balances recurring and instant culture sources while providing a safe one-time normalization of accumulated culture points for existing worlds.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Preview-first culture normalization
The system SHALL preview every affected player and the resulting balance without changing data unless an explicit apply mode is requested.

#### Scenario: Preview normalization
- **WHEN** an operator runs the normalization without apply mode
- **THEN** the system lists affected players and makes no database changes

#### Scenario: Apply normalization
- **WHEN** an operator explicitly runs the normalization in apply mode
- **THEN** the system caps each affected player's culture balance and reports the change

### Requirement: Preserve one additional village threshold
The normalization SHALL leave each regular player with no more than the slow-curve culture requirement for one village beyond their currently owned village count, and SHALL not increase balances below that requirement.

#### Scenario: Player has surplus culture
- **WHEN** a player owns three villages and has more culture than the slow requirement for four villages
- **THEN** the player's culture balance is reduced to the slow requirement for four villages

#### Scenario: Player is below the cap
- **WHEN** a player's culture balance is below the slow requirement for one additional village
- **THEN** the player's balance remains unchanged

### Requirement: Repeatable normalization
The normalization SHALL be safe to run repeatedly without reducing an already normalized balance further.

#### Scenario: Apply is repeated
- **WHEN** the operator applies normalization more than once without intervening culture gains above the cap
- **THEN** every subsequent run reports no balance changes

### Requirement: Reduced passive culture production
The system SHALL credit and display 25 percent of the raw culture production generated by village fields and buildings, using one authoritative account-wide calculation.

#### Scenario: Account has multiple villages
- **WHEN** an account's villages have a combined raw production of 5575 culture points per day
- **THEN** recurring village production contributes 1394 culture points per day after rounding

#### Scenario: Culture is credited daily
- **WHEN** the daily culture job credits an account
- **THEN** it credits the same balanced production shown in the culture interface

### Requirement: Balanced culture helmets
Equipped culture helmets SHALL contribute 25, 100, or 200 culture points per day for the Gladiator, Tribune, or Consul helmet respectively while the hero is alive.

#### Scenario: Consul helmet is equipped
- **WHEN** a living hero equips the Consul helmet
- **THEN** the account-wide daily culture calculation includes 200 helmet points

### Requirement: Celebration rewards remain fixed
Town Hall celebrations SHALL continue to grant 500 culture points for a small celebration and 2000 culture points for a great celebration.

#### Scenario: Great celebration completes
- **WHEN** a valid great celebration finishes
- **THEN** the account receives exactly 2000 culture points

### Requirement: Artwork uses balanced daily production
Each artwork SHALL grant the account's balanced daily culture production, subject to the existing maximum of 5000 points per artwork.

#### Scenario: Artwork is used
- **WHEN** the balanced account production is 1594 culture points per day
- **THEN** one artwork immediately grants 1594 culture points

### Requirement: Artwork has an account cooldown
The system SHALL permit at most one artwork consumption per account during any rolling 24-hour period and SHALL reject additional attempts without consuming an item or granting culture.

#### Scenario: Second artwork during cooldown
- **WHEN** an account tries to use another artwork less than 24 hours after a successful use
- **THEN** no artwork is consumed and no culture is granted

#### Scenario: Cooldown has elapsed
- **WHEN** at least 24 hours have passed since the account's last successful artwork use
- **THEN** the account may consume one artwork normally
